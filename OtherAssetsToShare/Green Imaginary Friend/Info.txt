A planned entity that can be found in a rare room.

The room contains a side room with a window, a sliding metal door and a table with the Green Imaginary Friend Remote.

The entity acts basically the same as the Imaginary Friend from Pressure.
- There distance 40 meter from the player and visable through walls
- They talk every 30-100 seconds
- They have a good chance of alerting the player of any entities nearby

They aren't very happy that there stuck with you though and most of there lines are complaining and annoyed.
